Financial Calculation Principles

1. Cash Balance: Derived strictly as All-time Incomes − All-time Expenses.

2. Investment Value: Calculated as Quantity × Current Unit Price for Stocks, Mutual Funds, Fixed Deposits, and Gold.

3. Total Assets: Cash Balance + Total Investment Portfolio Value. (Avoids double-counting capital transfers).

4. Total Liabilities: Sum of active debt and loan balances recorded in this module.

5. Net Worth: Total Assets − Total Liabilities using exact Python Decimal precision.
